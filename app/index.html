Make it part of your day

Know when to pray

See the five daily prayer times and a countdown to the next prayer. Choose your city or use your location. Once it’s saved, Waqt calculates prayer times on your device, even offline.

Wake up for Fajr

Set an alarm before Fajr and choose a math or QR challenge to help you get out of bed. System wake-up alarms require iOS or iPadOS 26 or later and alarm permission.

Plan for Ramadan and beyond

Set Suhoor and Tahajjud alarms relative to Fajr. Add personal reminders for extra prayers and check the next prayer from a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget.

Seven days to try Waqt

Try all features for seven days. After the trial, a one-time lifetime purchase is required to keep using Waqt, including its alerts and widgets. The trial ends automatically without a charge.
