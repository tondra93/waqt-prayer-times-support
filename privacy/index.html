Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Waqt iPhone and iPad app handles information. Effective November 10, 2026.

Information on your device

Waqt calculates prayer times on your device. It stores your saved location, settings, reminders, and wake-up code locally. The app has no Waqt account, advertising, analytics, or tracking. The developer does not receive this app data.

Location and city search

If you use automatic location or search for a city, coordinates or search terms may be sent to Apple’s location and mapping services to identify a place or time zone. Location permission is optional. You can choose a city manually.

Alarms, notifications, and camera

These permissions are optional and can be changed in your device settings. The camera reads the QR code on your device. Waqt does not save or upload camera images.

Trial and purchases

Apple processes the trial and lifetime purchase through StoreKit. Waqt uses Apple’s purchase records to check and restore access. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Support messages and deletion

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include. It is used to answer and manage your request. Contact support to request deletion of your correspondence. You can remove local app data by deleting Waqt. Apple manages its own purchase records.
